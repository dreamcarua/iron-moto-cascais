01 · Why a pre-purchase inspection

Why a pre-purchase inspection

A used motorcycle hides its history well. A fresh wash, a polished tank and a confident seller can cover a tired engine, a straightened frame, or a charging fault that surfaces a week after the sale. By the time you feel it on the road, the money is gone and the seller has moved on. A pre-purchase inspection removes the guesswork. We assess the real mechanical and electrical condition of the bike, separate cosmetic wear from costly problems, and tell you what it will take to make it right. The fee is a fraction of a single avoidable repair — and far less than buying the wrong bike outright. Independent and unbiased — this is the whole point. We work only for the buyer. We don't sell motorcycles, we don't take a margin on the deal, and we have no relationship with the seller. Our only job is to give you the truth about the machine. That independence is what separates a real inspection from a seller's reassurance. Buying in Portugal as an expat? The used market here moves on local knowledge, local language and local history — three things that are hard to read from the outside. We're your technical eyes on the ground. Our team works in English, Portuguese, Russian and Ukrainian, so nothing is lost in translation with the seller and no detail slips past because of a language gap. If you're searching for an English-speaking motorcycle inspection in Portugal, this is it. For the broader service flow, see our expat service hub. 02 · The differentiator: videoscope cylinder inspection + compression test

The differentiator: videoscope cylinder inspection + compression test

Key differentiator

Most sellers — and many workshops — never look inside the engine. They listen to it idle, glance at the oil, and call it good. We go further, because the engine is where the real money lives. We run a compression test to measure what each cylinder is actually doing. Compression tells the honest story of piston rings, valves and head sealing — the wear you cannot see or hear from outside, and the difference between a healthy engine and an expensive rebuild waiting to happen. Then we go inside. Using a videoscope (borescope), we pass a camera into the cylinder and look directly at the bore and piston crown — checking for scoring, wear, carbon build-up and damage. You see it on screen, and you keep the footage. For most buyers it's the first time they've ever seen the inside of an engine they were about to buy. It's the single most revealing test we run, and it's the reason riders bring us bikes before they sign. This is the standout. A seller can hide a lot on the surface. Inside the cylinder, the engine tells the truth.

03 · What we inspect

What we inspect

Our pre-purchase inspection is a structured, 10-point check covering everything that decides whether a used motorcycle is a smart buy or a costly mistake. We don't invent faults and we don't inflate concerns — we report what's there, with photos.

External / visual inspection

Overall condition, originality and consistency. We look for mismatched or replaced parts, signs of neglect, evidence of a hard life, and details that don't add up against the bike's claimed age and mileage. This is the first read on how the machine was treated.

Engine, compression test & internal cylinder inspection (videoscope)

Cold start behaviour, running condition, oil state, and the two tests most checks skip — a compression test on each cylinder and a direct look inside with a videoscope. This is where a tired or damaged engine reveals itself before it becomes your problem. (See section 02.)

Leaks & seepage

Oil, coolant, fork seals and gaskets. A weeping seal is cheap; a leaking head or case can be a major repair. We identify where it's coming from and how serious it is.

Brakes

Pad and disc wear, brake lines, fluid condition and actual function front and rear. Brakes are a safety system, not a negotiation footnote — we tell you what's worn and what's due.

Suspension

Forks, shock, bearings, travel and leaks. Worn or blown suspension ruins how a bike rides and can be expensive to put right — better to know before you pay.

Frame & crash damage

Frame straightness, weld and repair signs, and evidence of drops or accidents. A bike that's been down hard can look perfect and still be compromised. This check protects you from the worst surprises.

Electrical

Battery and charging system, lights, electronics, fault codes, and wiring and connector condition including corrosion. Electrical gremlins are among the most frustrating and time-consuming faults to chase — we flag them before you own them.

Tyres

Tread depth, wear pattern, age (date codes) and damage. Old or damaged tyres are both a safety issue and an immediate cost to factor into the price.

Final drive — chain, belt or cardan

Wear, adjustment and condition of the chain and sprockets, drive belt, or shaft drive. A neglected final drive is a real cost and often a sign of how the rest of the bike was maintained.

List of recommendations

The written deliverable: what's good, what needs attention now, and what to budget for next. Clear, prioritised, and honest. (See section 06.)

FAQ

FAQ

How long does a pre-purchase inspection take?⌄

A full inspection typically takes around 1.5 to 2 hours, depending on the bike and whether it's at our Cascais workshop or on-site. We don't rush it — the point is to check everything that matters, including the compression test and the videoscope look inside the engine.

Can you inspect the bike on-site, not just at your workshop?⌄

Yes. We inspect at our Cascais workshop or on-site at the seller's location — we travel. On-site is ideal when the motorcycle isn't roadworthy or the seller won't move it. The travel charge is agreed separately according to distance, and confirmed before you book.

Do I get a written report?⌄

Yes. You receive a written report with a prioritised list of recommendations, photos, and videoscope footage including the internal cylinder video. It's evidence you can use to buy, negotiate or walk away.

What if the seller refuses the internal cylinder inspection?⌄

A seller who won't allow a compression test or a videoscope check is worth a second thought. We'll still complete every other point of the inspection and tell you clearly what we could and couldn't verify — so you can weigh the risk with open eyes.

Do you inspect any brand of motorcycle?⌄

Yes. We inspect every brand and type we service — Harley-Davidson, BMW, Ducati, Suzuki, Honda and beyond, from sport bikes to cruisers, classics and customs.

Is an inspection worth it compared to just buying the bike?⌄

Almost always. The fee is a fraction of a single avoidable repair, and far less than buying the wrong motorcycle. An independent inspection turns a gamble into an informed decision — and often pays for itself in the price you negotiate.
