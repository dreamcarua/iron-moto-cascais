Motorcycle Tyre Fitting & Wheel Balancing in Cascais

Car tyre fitting is not motorcycle tyre fitting. A car shop is built for car wheels — different machines, different geometry, different risks. At Iron Custom Motors in São Domingos de Rana, Cascais, we run motorcycle-specific equipment that is unmatched in the region and built for moto wheels only. We fit tyres up to 30" rim and up to 400 mm width, and we balance any motorcycle wheel up to those limits — without removing your brake discs or hubs. 01 · What a car tyre shop can't do

What a car tyre shop can't do

A car tyre bay simply isn't designed for what a motorcycle wheel demands. Our machines are purpose-built for moto wheels, so we change tyres without removing your brake discs or hubs — protecting the components that car-oriented equipment so often forces off the wheel. We work confidently with spoked wheels, with tube and tubeless setups, and with the delicate rims that generic shops would rather not touch. This is the difference between a tyre fitted on car machinery and a tyre fitted by a motorcycle specialist. See the process and you'll understand why riders bring us the wheels other shops decline. 02 · Any wheel. Up to 30" and 400 mm

Any wheel. Up to 30" and 400 mm

We service motorcycle wheels from 10" to 30", up to 400 mm wide — and very little falls outside that. Spoked and vintage wheels, classic restorations, Harley and custom fat tyres, tube and tubeless: all are routine here. Wide rubber that defeats most shops is everyday work on our motorcycle tyre changer. Whatever you ride — sport, naked, touring, chopper or full custom — your wheel meets equipment that was made for it.

03 · Wheel balancing for heavy & oversized wheels

Wheel balancing for heavy & oversized wheels

We balance any motorcycle wheel up to 30" and 400 mm, including the heavy, oversized and custom wheels that smaller setups can't handle. Correct balancing is not cosmetic. An unbalanced wheel sends vibration through the bars and footpegs, wears your tyre unevenly, stresses bearings, and undermines stability at speed. On a motorcycle, where contact patch and confidence are everything, a properly balanced wheel is a safety component. We balance to a true result so the bike feels planted from idle to motorway.

We also source any rim or wheel brand to your specification. Whether you want a specific compound, a hard-to-find size or a complete custom wheel, you tell us the spec and we bring it in. One specialist, from the tyre order to the finished, balanced wheel.

FAQ

What is the maximum wheel size you can handle?⌄

We fit tyres up to a 30" rim and up to 400 mm width, and we service wheels from 10" to 30". Almost any motorcycle wheel is within our range.

Can you handle spoked, vintage and Harley fat tyres?⌄

Yes. Spoked wheels, vintage and classic restorations, Harley and custom fat tyres in both tube and tubeless are routine work on our motorcycle-specific equipment.

Do you remove the brake discs to change a tyre?⌄

No. Our machines let us change tyres without removing your brake discs or hubs, protecting components that car-oriented equipment often forces off.

Can I bring my own tyre?⌄

Yes. We fit customer-supplied tyres. Bring the tyre and your wheel or bike, and we'll handle the fitting and balancing.

Can you order a specific tyre or rim brand and size?⌄

Yes. As an official representative we can order any tyre brand and any rim or wheel brand, any size, made to order, with fast delivery to your specification.

How much does fitting cost?⌄

Can you balance large or heavy wheels?⌄

Yes. We balance any motorcycle wheel up to 30" and 400 mm, including heavy, oversized and custom wheels that smaller shops can't take on.

Do you offer a B2B service for other workshops?⌄
