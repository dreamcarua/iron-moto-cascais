FAQ

FAQ.

Is there an English-speaking motorcycle mechanic near Lisbon or Cascais?

Yes. Iron Custom Motors in São Domingos de Rana, Cascais, works in English, Portuguese, Russian and Ukrainian, and gives a written estimate before any work begins. It serves riders across Cascais, Estoril, Oeiras, Sintra and Greater Lisbon.

Do I need a periodic motorcycle inspection (IPO) in Portugal?

On mainland Portugal, no — the periodic motorcycle inspection was abolished under Lei 25/2025. The Azores remain an exception. These rules have changed before, so confirm the current position with IMT or an official inspection centre.

Can you check a used motorcycle before I buy it?

Yes. Our independent pre-purchase inspection includes a compression test and an internal cylinder check with a videoscope, plus a written report — from €150. We work only for the buyer.

Can you help with an imported or foreign-plate motorcycle?

Yes, on the technical side — a condition check and first service after import, and honest advice on what the bike needs. We don't handle registration paperwork, but we'll prepare the machine and point you the right way.

What languages do you speak?

English, Portuguese, Russian and Ukrainian — all spoken by our team, with written estimates in your language.

Which areas do you serve?

Cascais, Estoril, Oeiras, Sintra, Lisbon and the wider Greater Lisbon area, from our workshop in São Domingos de Rana.
