### 02 · How a project is born
**1. Conversation.** A sketch session with your references on the table. We look at the base bike and tell you honestly what is feasible and what is not worth the money.
**2. Concept.** One direction, drawn, with the parts that define it named. This is the moment to change your mind cheaply.
**3. Engineering on paper.** Frame, geometry, ergonomics, mechanical and electronic systems, a parts list, a timeline with milestones.
**4. Build.** In-house, with trusted partner shops for specific work. Each stage is documented, so you follow the progress rather than wait for the reveal.
**5. Refine.** Real-world testing, then adjustments. The bike leaves when it rides the way the concept said it would.

### 03 · Timeline and budget
There is no price list for custom builds. Instead:
The scope is written down — what changes on the bike and what stays. The budget and the timeline are agreed in writing before the build starts. Milestones mark the build, so you always know where the project stands. If the scope changes along the way, the document changes first, then the work.
The consultation is free, always, and it is the first step of every project.

### FAQ
- **How much does a custom motorcycle cost?** There is no fixed price. The budget depends on the base bike, the scope and the parts, and you receive it in writing after the consultation, together with the timeline. The consultation itself is free. Nothing starts before you have agreed to both.
- **How long does a build take?** It depends on the scope, so we do not name a duration before the project is defined. The timeline and its milestones are written down before work begins.
- **Can you build on my own bike?** Yes. The consultation includes a technical assessment of your motorcycle and an honest answer on what it can carry. If a different base would serve the idea better, we say so.
