FAQ

C-Way dealer questions

Are you the official C-Way dealer in Portugal?

Yes. Iron Custom Motors is the exclusive official C-Way dealer in Portugal. Every product is ordered directly from the manufacturer.

Are the parts genuine and covered by the manufacturer warranty?

Yes. All C-Way products are genuine, supplied directly from the factory in Ukraine and carry the full official manufacturer warranty.

Do you install the products, and is the installation guaranteed?

Yes. Our Gold Wing specialists install everything in-house, and we provide our own warranty on both the workmanship and the materials.
